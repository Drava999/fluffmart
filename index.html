How store credit works
🎁
How you earn it — $3 back for every $100 you spend, automatically. Refer a friend and you get $3 once they spend $15+. Sell us cards and you can take store credit instead of cash (we pay a better rate for credit).
🛒
How you spend it — at checkout, toggle "Use store credit" and it comes off your total before you PayNow. Partial is fine: if credit covers $4 of a $10 order, you just pay $6. If it covers everything, there's nothing to pay — we verify and your cards go straight into your parcel.
↩️
Refunds — whatever you paid with credit comes back as credit, and whatever you paid by PayNow can come back as either. Credit lands instantly; cash refunds are sent by hand — just ask in Help & Support.
ℹ️
Good to know — credit never expires, but it can't be withdrawn as cash. If an order is cancelled or times out, any credit you used on it comes straight back to your balance.
