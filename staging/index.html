How store credit works
🎁
How you earn it. $3 back for every $100 you pay. Refer a friend and you BOTH get $3 once they spend $15+. Sell us cards and take credit instead of cash. we pay a better rate for credit.

How you spend it. Toggle "Use store credit" at checkout and it comes off before you PayNow. Partial is fine: $4 of credit on a $10 order means you pay $6. If it covers everything, there's nothing to pay at all.

Refunds. Whatever you paid with credit comes back as credit. Whatever you paid by PayNow can come back either way: credit lands instantly, cash we send by hand.

Good to know. Credit never expires, but it can't be cashed out. If an order is cancelled or times out, any credit you used comes straight back.
